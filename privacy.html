Privacy Policy

Boxters — Hex Word Puzzle Game

Effective Date: August 6, 2026

Boxters is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data is stored, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

Data Collection

Boxters does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

  • No personal data is collected, processed, or sent to any server.
  • No advertising SDKs are integrated.
  • No account creation or internet connection is required to play.
  • No analytics or tracking tools are used in this app.

Contact Form

If you choose to use the Contact form within the app, the name, email, and message you enter are sent to Formspree for delivery. This data is not stored by Boxters and is subject to Formspree's privacy policy. Submitting the form is entirely optional.

Local Storage

Your game progress (player name, scores, level progress, and settings) is stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences. This data:

  • Never leaves your device.
  • Is accessible only to you within this app.
  • Is not shared across devices or apps.
  • Can be cleared at any time (see below).

Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Access: Your data is stored on your device and visible to you at any time.
  • Deletion: Clear all saved data by going to Android Settings > Apps > Boxters > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Portability: No cloud data exists to export — all data is local.

Children's Privacy

Boxters does not collect any personal data from any users, including children.

Changes to This Policy

Any updates to this privacy policy will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.
